# Customer Advocacy Simulation

This document outlines the critical questions and concerns from the perspective of both clients and service providers (maids) for the HydraClean platform.

---

## As a Client, Seeking Cleaning Services

My primary concern is **safety and trust**. I am letting a stranger into my home, around my family and my belongings.

1.  **"How do I know this person is safe?"**
    *   What does your "vetting" process *actually* involve? Is it just an email check, or have you performed a legitimate, third-party criminal background check? I need to know you've done more due diligence than I could do myself on social media.

2.  **"What happens if something goes wrong?"**
    *   If the cleaner accidentally breaks my expensive TV or damages my property, who pays for it? Do you have platform insurance, or am I expected to chase down the individual cleaner for damages? If there's no protection, it's a non-starter.
    *   What about theft? If something goes missing, what is the exact process? How do you support me?

3.  **"How do I know I'll get a quality cleaning?"**
    *   Reviews are a good start, but what if the service I receive is terrible and doesn't match the reviews? Can I get a refund? Is there a "satisfaction guarantee"? What prevents a cleaner from doing a poor job, taking my money, and facing no consequences?

4.  **"What's my recourse if the cleaner is a no-show?"**
    *   I've planned my day around this appointment. If the cleaner simply doesn't arrive, what happens? Does the platform help me find an immediate replacement? Am I compensated for my wasted time?

5.  **"How is my privacy protected?"**
    *   Does the cleaner have my home address and phone number permanently after one job? What prevents them from contacting me outside the app or showing up unannounced? I need to know my personal data is secure and only used for the service booked.

---

## As a Maid, Providing My Services

My primary concern is **my safety and my income**. I am going into a stranger's home alone, and I'm counting on this platform for my livelihood.

1.  **"How do you ensure my safety?"**
    *   You're sending me to an unvetted client's home. What if the situation feels unsafe or the client is inappropriate? Is there an emergency contact feature or a "panic button" in the app? How do I know you have my back if something goes wrong?

2.  **"How do you guarantee I will be paid for my work?"**
    *   What happens if I complete a 4-hour job and the client refuses to pay or claims the work was poor just to get it for free? Does the platform hold the money in escrow? Will you fight for my payment, or am I on my own?

3.  **"What happens if a client cancels last minute?"**
    *   I've turned down other jobs to reserve this time slot. If a client cancels an hour before the appointment, I've lost income for the day. Is there a cancellation fee that compensates me for my time?

4.  **"How are unfair ratings handled?"**
    *   My rating is my reputation. What if a client gives me a 1-star review for a reason that's unfair or out of my control (e.g., they were unhappy about your platform's fees)? Can I dispute a rating? A few bad, unfair ratings could destroy my business.

5.  **"How do you handle scope creep?"**
    *   What if I arrive and the home is far dirtier than described, requiring much more time and effort? Am I allowed to adjust the price with the client's approval through the app? What if they refuse? Am I forced to do double the work for the original price or risk a bad review for canceling?
