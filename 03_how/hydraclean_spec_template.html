Project Overview

The Problem

Traditional cleaning services create barriers between clients and cleaners, leading to higher costs and less personalized service

Our Solution

Direct connection platform that eliminates intermediaries while ensuring safety and quality

Value Proposition

For Clients: Better service, transparent pricing, flexible scheduling

For Maids: Higher earnings, schedule control, direct client relationships

Core Features

🔐 Secure Authentication

Dual user registration with email verification and identity confirmation for maids

🔍 Smart Discovery

Location-based search with detailed profiles, ratings, and availability

📅 Booking Management

Request-accept workflow with calendar integration and confirmation system

💬 In-App Messaging

Secure communication between clients and maids after booking confirmation

💳 Transparent Payments

Clear pricing with platform fees, secure processing, and automated payouts

⭐ Review System

Mutual rating system for both clients and maids to build trust and quality
