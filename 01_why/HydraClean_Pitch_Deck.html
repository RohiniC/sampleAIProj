The Problem: A Broken System

For Clients

  • Struggling to find trusted, independent help.
  • Tired of impersonal, inflexible service companies.
  • Lack of transparency in pricing and service.

For Maids

  • Losing significant earnings to intermediary fees.
  • Lack of control over their schedule and clients.
  • Difficulty finding consistent, direct work.

Our Solution: Direct Connection

A Platform Built on Trust

HydraClean facilitates direct, transparent connections between independent house maids and clients, bypassing traditional intermediaries.

Core Value Proposition

Empowerment for maids, transparency and choice for clients.

The MVP: Key Features

  • User Profiles & Vetting
  • Direct Search & Discovery
  • Simple Booking & Scheduling
  • In-App Messaging
  • Secure, Transparent Payments
  • Two-Way Reviews & Ratings

Advocating for Our Clients

We've listened to your primary concern: Safety and Trust.

  • "How do I know this person is safe?"
    Our MVP includes initial vetting, with clear plans for comprehensive background checks.
  • "What if something goes wrong?"
    We're building a system with clear policies and exploring platform insurance for peace of mind.
  • "Will I get a quality cleaning?"
    Our two-way rating system ensures accountability and our platform will include a satisfaction guarantee.

Advocating for Our Maids

We've listened to your primary concern: Your Safety and Your Income.

  • "How do you ensure my safety?"
    Client ratings and in-app communication logs are just the start. We're building safety-first features.
  • "How do you guarantee I will be paid?"
    Our secure payment system ensures you're paid promptly for your work, with dispute resolution support.
  • "What about last-minute cancellations?"
    Our platform will enforce a fair cancellation policy that compensates you for your reserved time.

Measuring MVP Success

User Acquisition & Activation

Tracking the number of registered and active clients and maids.

Booking Completion Rate

Ensuring that requests turn into completed, paid jobs.

Retention & Feedback

Measuring repeat usage and gathering qualitative feedback for the next iteration.
